Comparison Regimes (Final Clarification)

1) Stage 1 Fine-Tuned (task-specific)
- Separate model per task, trained/fine-tuned for that task.
- Classification: ViT / EfficientNet / ResNet
- Detection: YOLOv8 / Faster R-CNN / RetinaNet
- Segmentation: DeepLabV3+ / U-Net / Mask R-CNN

2) Stage 1 Zero-Shot / Foundation
- Foundation models used without waste-specific fine-tuning.
- Classification: CLIP
- Detection: Grounding DINO
- Segmentation: SAM

3) Stage 2 Zero-Shot (unified model)
- Single model: microsoft/Florence-2-large-ft
- No LoRA adapter, no task training on waste data.
- Same prompts used for all tasks: <CAPTION>, <OD>, <REFERRING_EXPRESSION_SEGMENTATION>

4) Stage 2 Fine-Tuned (unified model)
- Single model: Florence-2 + LoRA adapter
- Adapter path: finetuned/florence2_unified_multitask_lora
- One model handles classification, detection, and segmentation.

Current Insight Summary

- In-domain peak performance is still best with Stage 1 fine-tuned specialists.
- Cross-domain classification is best with Stage 2 fine-tuned unified model.
- Cross-domain detection is strongest with Stage 2 Florence (zero-shot and fine-tuned), both above Stage 1 baseline.
- Segmentation quality from Stage 2 is moderate and below best Stage 1 specialist segmentation.
